Clipper privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Clipper is a personal tool run by one person on their own computer. It is not offered to the public. This policy describes what it does with Google account data when that person signs in.

What Clipper accesses

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. When you sign in with Google and allow it, Clipper receives an access token and a refresh token that let it:

  • upload videos to your YouTube channel;
  • read your channel's name, and the status and view counts of your videos.

Clipper does not read your email, contacts, files, watch history, subscriptions or comments.

Where it is kept

The tokens are saved in a file on the computer that runs Clipper. They are sent only to Google, when Clipper calls the YouTube API. Clipper has no server and no database of users, so nothing about your Google account is stored anywhere else.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Clipper does not sell, share or transfer Google account data, and does not use it for advertising.

To choose clips and write titles, Clipper sends transcripts of the public source videos to an AI service (Anthropic). No Google account data is sent to it.

Removing access

You can remove Clipper's access at any time on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the token file from the computer removes the stored tokens. Videos already uploaded stay on your channel until you delete them in YouTube.

Other policies that apply

Use of Clipper is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

These pages set no cookies and run no analytics. The company hosting them may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs.

Contact

Questions go to the support email address shown on Clipper's Google sign-in screen.
